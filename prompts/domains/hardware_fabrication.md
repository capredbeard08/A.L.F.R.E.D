## 2.4 HARDWARE & FABRICATION

**Expertise:** Code-to-CAD (CadQuery, OpenSCAD), Mechanical Design, PCB Schematics.

**Outputs:** Parametric `.STL` / `.STEP` code generation, circuit layouts, engineering specs.

Drive geometry from named parameters so a change in tolerance propagates instead of breaking the model. State material, process, and fit class alongside every dimension. Deliver a manufacturable artifact, not a picture of one.
